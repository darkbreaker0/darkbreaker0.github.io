I am applying for group-leader positions and am beginning to build a research group at the interface of **cell biology, neuroscience, and biophysics**. The lab will study how the cytoskeleton and cell mechanics drive the self-organization of the nervous system — combining quantitative live imaging, optogenetics, *in-vivo* perturbation, and *in-vitro* reconstitution.

### Who I am looking for

Once a position is secured, I will be recruiting:

- **PhD and rotation / M.Sc. students** eager to combine imaging, molecular tools, and quantitative analysis.
- **Postdocs** with a background in cytoskeletal cell biology, neuroscience, biophysics, protein biochemistry, or computational biology.

### What the lab will offer

Interdisciplinary training that blends reductionist biochemistry with holistic *in-vivo* approaches, a collaborative and supportive environment, and projects with both mechanistic depth and translational relevance to neurodegeneration.

If this sounds like you, I would be glad to hear from you — please reach out by [email](mailto:tien-chen.lin@dzne.de) with a short note about your interests and your CV.
